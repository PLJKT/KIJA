# Cikarang vs Kendal Operating Profitability Analysis (2021-2025)

Source: KIJA Audited Financial Reports 2021-2025, Note 34 (Segment Information) & Note 24 (Non-controlling interests). All figures IDR bn.

## Methodology

1. Take Real estate segment PBT as reported in Note 34.
2. Subtract "Other expenses - Net" positive line at Real estate segment (this is inter-segment income — mainly dividends received by parent from Power/Services subsidiaries and inter-company gains; it is 100% eliminated in consolidation, see Note 34 elimination column).
3. Result = Real estate operating PBT.
4. Kendal is 51%-owned JV with Sembcorp; NCI = 49%. Kendal net profit = NCI / 0.49.
5. Kendal PBT ≈ Kendal net / 0.97 (property final tax ~3%).
6. Cikarang operating PBT = Real estate operating PBT − Kendal PBT.

## Year-by-year (IDR bn)

| Item | 2021 | 2022 | 2023 | 2024 | 2025 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Real estate segment PBT | 560 | 626 | 1,436 | 1,876 | 2,016 |
| Less: inter-segment other income (eliminated) | (395) | (360) | (844) | (1,174) | (1,381) |
| Real estate operating PBT | 165 | 266 | 592 | 702 | 635 |
| Kendal NCI (reported) | 92 | 104 | 225 | 407 | 435 |
| Kendal net = NCI/49% | 188 | 212 | 459 | 831 | 888 |

| **Cikarang operating PBT** | **(29)** | **+47** | **+119** | **(155)** | **(281)** |

## Conclusion

- Cikarang (old Jababeka estate) land-sale business is roughly breakeven-to-loss-making over 2021-2025: -29, +47, +119, -155, -281 bn.
- Cikarang's reported "net profit" (565→639→1,423→1,859→2,001 bn) is inflated by inter-segment other income that grew from 395 bn (2021) to 1,381 bn (2025) — mainly dividends from Power/Services subsidiaries to the parent. These are 100% eliminated in consolidation.
- Kendal is the growth engine: operating PBT grew 194→916 bn, 4.7x over 5 years.
- Implication: don't read Real estate segment net profit as Cikarang operating profit. Strip out the inter-segment other income; the old estate is at end-of-life, growth is Kendal + recurring infrastructure (Power/Services).
